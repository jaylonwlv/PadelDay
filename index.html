Will it fit my padel paddle?

The bag is built around a racket sleeve in the drawstring top, and a padel paddle rides in the top the same way, with the handle out. Padel paddles are wider and thicker than tennis rackets, so if you're unsure about your model, email us before you order and we'll help you check.

Which color should I pick?

Clean white is the brighter, cuter look. Moody black hides court dust and goes with everything.

Every bag comes with 5 overgrips in your bag's color, free. They wrap over the handle of tennis rackets, pickleball paddles, and padel paddles, and regular players usually swap them every few weeks.

No. You get the bag and 5 overgrips. The rackets and the bag charm in the photos are there to show how it looks in use.
